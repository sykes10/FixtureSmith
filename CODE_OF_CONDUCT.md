# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FixtureSmith a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling,
and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when publicly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or private security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a
safe, productive community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
possible and without retaliation.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
